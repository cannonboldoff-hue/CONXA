Clarifying Questions Audit

Before Build Studio makes a workflow it asks the person a few questions. This page shows what it asks today, where it goes silent, and a way to decide what to ask that does not depend on someone having thought of the situation first.

8 of 15question kinds are never asked for a recording that went through the new flow.
1model call per recording. Nothing is re-checked after an answer.
7 of 12things a finished workflow needs are not asked about at all (my reading of the code).

How a question gets made today

There are two question generators, one before the compile and one after. Both are catalogues: a fixed list of question types, each fired by a pattern someone wrote. A situation nobody listed produces no question.

Eight kinds of question are switched off

The filter in compile_flow.py keeps five kinds and its comment says "every gap was asked before the build". Only two of the other ten were. Dropdowns, dates, optional fields, missing records, outputs and "how do we know it worked" are never asked.

What a finished workflow needs, and what gets asked

Fixing the filter restores old questions. It does not make the system general. A reusable, deterministic workflow has a fixed anatomy of twelve slots, and today's six question effects touch only some of them.

A loop built on unknowns, not patterns

Derive questions from the slots the finished workflow needs. Each open slot becomes an unknown with hypotheses, evidence and impact. A model proposes unknowns. Code decides whether each one is worth a person's attention, and every answer feeds back before the next decision.

Deciding whether to ask

Ask when a wrong guess is likely and expensive. Otherwise assume and show it, confirm with a default, or leave it for the test. Safety cases skip the formula and always ask.

How to know it is working

Today the eval script counts how often the validator dropped something and says it "judges nothing". These measure whether the questions were useful and whether needed ones were missing.

Too many questions?

  • Vacuity rate. Share of questions where every choice produces the same edits.
  • Guess accuracy. Pre-selected choice against the final answer, grouped by stated confidence.
  • Skip and abandon rate per question kind.
  • Questions per workflow against steps and inputs.

Anything missed?

  • Slot recall against recordings labelled with the slots a correct workflow needs.
  • Miss rate. Edits and failed tests that a question could have prevented.
  • Escape rate. Customer-run failures traced to a slot nobody settled.
  • Silent cuts. Rows and candidates dropped by a cap, counted and shown.

Order of work

PHASE 0

Fix defects

Pass chat in. Keep notes without a task description. Re-open the eight dropped kinds. Count silent cuts.

PHASE 1

One ledger

Express today's kinds as unknowns. Add the vacuity check and slot-keyed dedupe.

PHASE 2

Decide by value

Scoring, assume and defer, the assumptions list, the readiness report.

PHASE 3

Close the loop

Re-evaluate after answers, auditor pass, answer memory, sandbox replay as evidence.

PHASE 4

Measure

Gold-slot corpus, selection fixtures, miss log wired to test and run failures.

Decisions needed: whether the filter was intended or drift. How many interruptions are acceptable. Whether a sandbox replay before asking is allowed, since it touches the rule that testing waits for answers. Who labels the gold recordings.
