## Findings (suspected layer only)
- F1 (S2) Sign-in expiry shows up as "The element this step needs wasn't found" on the saucedemo skill (runs 4 and 15.3/15.4 attempts: failure page is the login page "You can only access '/inventory.html' when you are logged in"). Sometimes Studio instead pauses with the proper "A sign-in expired during the test" card (before the run, first time), but when the session dies mid-run, or a sign-in was saved with "Save anyway" after "The signed-in page didn't confirm sign-in", the run walks into the login page and fails with a misleading element-not-found message. Layer: runtime `run.js::detectFault` / login-decision signals (`login_prove_failed` in the log), Studio failure wording (`failure_summary.py`). The saucedemo saved sign-in also lasted only ~10 minutes in this session. Because of this, the named known-good skills P-27/P-29/WF-4 Leg B could not be used for the endurance loop (ParaBank skill failed too: `Click the account that received the money` after app data drift, evidence `shop-attempt/` and `bank-fail.png`).
- F2 (S2) Chat history after leaving and returning to a workflow during a test shows stale/missing result cards (see 15.10). Layer: renderer conversation thread rebuild.
- F3 (S3) A 30 s outage mid-run is silently absorbed (run slower, passed) while a 75 s outage fails plainly. Fine behavior, but there is no hint to the person that the network was down; spec wording expected a failure. Layer: runtime wait budgets.
- F4 (S3) Pane message "Clicking in the page while it runs can throw the test off." contradicts the shield (clicks are blocked). Layer: renderer wording.
- F5 (S3) Killed browser tab headline is "Something went wrong at this step." with the real cause only in Technical details. Layer: `failure_summary.py`.
- F6 (S3) `CONXA_ACTION_TIMEOUT_MS=400` does not force a failure on short skills; the 15.2 sabotage value in the spec may not break them.

## UI/UX notes (non-technical user)
- Good: the offline banner, the "interrupted" card after quitting, "Test stopped by you ... 4 steps ran, up to ...", and "Couldn't connect to the website. Check your internet connection." are plain and name the step.
- Bad: element-not-found when the real cause is "you were logged out" (F1); a sign-in flow that says "The signed-in page didn't confirm sign-in against what was learned." (technical) with "Save anyway".
- During a long run the only progress text is "Step 4 of 6 - pausing before steps that can't be undone"; the 40 s wait after the Enter step has no explanation.
