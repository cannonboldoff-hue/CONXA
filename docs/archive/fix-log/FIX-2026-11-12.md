# Fix Log — 2026-11-12

**A test no longer fails just because the app opened a progress panel after a popup answer — 2026-11-12**
After a test answered a popup (for example "replace this file?"), it checked that the page had no popup at all. The app's own "uploading / upload complete" panel counted, so the test failed even though the answer worked, and the failure card pointed at the wrong step and the wrong browser tab. Now the test checks only that the popup it answered is gone, shows the right step and the right page when something fails, and no longer treats a "done" panel as a question. This makes tests of upload-style workflows pass, and failures point you at the real problem.
— 2026-11-12

**The browser you watch while testing no longer cuts off the right side — 2026-11-12**
While a test ran, the website was drawn too big for the browser space, so the right side of the page was cut off, unlike while recording. Now the page is shrunk to fit the space, just like in recording, and the test's clicks still land in the right place. You see the whole page while the test runs.
— 2026-11-12

**The "checking against your description" message is gone — 2026-11-12**
After a recording was turned into a workflow, the chat said it was "checking it against your description", even though descriptions are no longer asked for. It now just says "Checking your workflow". This keeps the message true to what you actually did.
— 2026-11-12
